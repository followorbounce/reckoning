Area

Areas are squares of lengths, so every area unit here is exact once its length is. The acre is a chain by a furlong (66 by 660 feet): a strip of ploughland one furlong long.

Convert area

Then and now

Land measures varied from place to place for centuries; the rood and the square perch survive only in old deeds.
