Weather · marine forecasts

Visibility

Visibility is the greatest distance at which a dark object can be seen and recognised against the sky. At sea it decides how vessels must navigate and which sound signals they must make.

Forecast terms

TermVisibility
Very poorless than 1000 metres
Poor1000 metres to 2 nautical miles
Moderate2 to 5 nautical miles
Goodmore than 5 nautical miles

Fog, mist and haze

FogVisibility below 1000 m because of water droplets in the air.
MistVisibility reduced by droplets but still 1000 m or more, with high humidity.
HazeVisibility reduced by dry particles such as dust or smoke.
Then and now

Older seamanship tables graded fog by the distance at which objects disappeared, from dense fog at about 50 yards to excellent visibility beyond 30 miles. Forecasts now use four plain terms with fixed limits.

In restricted visibility ships must sound the fog signals on the sound signals page.
