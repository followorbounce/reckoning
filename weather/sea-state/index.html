Weather · WMO code 3700

State of the sea

Observers on ships report the sea as one figure from 0 to 9. The heights are for well-developed wind waves in the open sea; the words come first and the heights are a guide.

CodeDescriptionHeight, m
0Calm (glassy)0
1Calm (rippled)0–0.1
2Smooth (wavelets)0.1–0.5
3Slight0.5–1.25
4Moderate1.25–2.5
5Rough2.5–4
6Very rough4–6
7High6–9
8Very high9–14
9Phenomenalover 14

Source: Manual on Codes, Volume I.1 (WMO-No. 306): code table 3700 (state of the sea) and 4677 (present weather). A height exactly on a boundary takes the lower code figure: 4 m is coded 5.

Sea and swell

Wind waves are raised by the wind blowing now; swell is wave energy that has travelled out of the area where it was raised, long and regular. A calm day can carry a heavy swell from a storm a thousand miles away.
