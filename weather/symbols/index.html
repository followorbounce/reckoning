Weather · WMO code 4677

Weather symbols

On a weather map each observing station reports “present weather” as a two-figure code, ww, from 00 to 99, drawn as a small sign. The signs combine a few elements: a dot for rain, a comma for drizzle, a star for snow, a triangle for showers.

Reading the signs

Intensity is counted in elements: one, two, three or four dots for slight to heavy. Elements stacked vertically mean intermittent; spread side by side, continuous. An inverted triangle beneath a sign makes it a shower.
