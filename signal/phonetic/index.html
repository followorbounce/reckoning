Phonetic alphabet

On a noisy radio B, D, E, P and T all sound alike. Each letter is therefore spoken as a word chosen to be recognisable to speakers of English, French and Spanish alike. The same words name the code flags.

Then and now

Before 1956 each service and country had its own list. The words Charlie, Mike, Victor and X-ray survived from the wartime American list into today’s alphabet.
