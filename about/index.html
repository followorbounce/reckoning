Method

How it is checked

A handbook is only useful if it is right. These are the rules this one follows, and the checks that ran when this copy was built.

Rules

  • Every unit is stored once, as an exact fraction of its SI unit wherever the unit is defined exactly, with the source of that definition.
  • Values are compared with reference values typed in separately from the NIST tables; the build stops on any mismatch.
  • Every area and volume formula is compared with a Monte Carlo measurement of the shape, made from a point-in-shape test written from the shape’s definition, not from its formula. Selected perimeters and surfaces are integrated numerically.
  • The formula shown on a page is the same expression that was checked; the page evaluates it live.
  • Old values are not deleted. They are shown as “then and now”, so old documents can still be read.
  • All text is new. The topics follow mid-century practical handbooks; no text or drawings are taken from them.

Review

Pages are reviewed through the lenses of the studio charter: mathematics (formulas, topology), physics and metrology (units and constants), history (then-and-now notes), teaching (one clear point per page) and design (honest scales, accessibility). These reviews are done by the author applying those lenses. An outside review by working practitioners has not happened yet; if you find an error, it will be corrected and the correction noted.
