Trigonometry

Sine and cosine are coordinates: walk an angle round a circle of radius 1, and the point you reach is (cos θ, sin θ). Everything else, including the tangent, the laws of sines and cosines and every navigator’s traverse table, follows from that picture.

Solve a triangle

Name the sides a, b, c and the opposite angles A, B, C. Any three facts that include a side fix the triangle, with one exception: two sides and an angle that is not between them (SSA) can give two different triangles, one, or none. That is the ambiguous case.

Given

The solver is the same code that the build checks: 2 000 random triangles are recovered from every kind of data, and the number of SSA solutions is compared with a ruler-and-compass construction.

Then and now

Navigators worked with printed tables of logarithms of sines, and of the haversine, hav θ = sin²(θ/2), which keeps every quantity positive and avoids sign errors. The same haversine formula computes the great-circle distances on the sailing page. Courses were once given in points (“west by south”); today they are three-figure bearings, 000° to 359°.
