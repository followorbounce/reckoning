Torus (anchor ring)

A circle of radius r swept round an axis at distance R: a ring, a doughnut, an O-ring.

Formulas

Pappus’s theorems: a shape swept round an axis gives a volume equal to its area times the distance its centre travels, here πr² × 2πR; the same for perimeter and surface.

Checked: the V, S formula agrees with a Monte Carlo measurement of the shape itself, made from its geometric definition, every time the site is built.

Topology lens

A solid with one hole through it, like a ring or a pipe. Its surface is a torus: Euler characteristic 0, genus 1. No stretching makes it a ball. More on the lens.
