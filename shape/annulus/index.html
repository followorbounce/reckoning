Annulus (ring)

The flat ring between two circles with the same centre, like a washer.

area A

Formulas

The big disc minus the hole. Written as π(R + r)(R − r) it is the mean circumference times the width: unroll the ring into a strip.

Checked: the A formula agrees with a Monte Carlo measurement of the shape itself, made from its geometric definition, every time the site is built.

Topology lens

A region with one hole. Its Euler characteristic is 0: it can be stretched into any other ring, but never into a disc without tearing. More on the lens.
