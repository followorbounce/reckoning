Formulas

Every slice parallel to the base is a copy of the base, so the volume is base area times height (Cavalieri’s principle).

Checked: the V, S formula agrees with a Monte Carlo measurement of the shape itself, made from its geometric definition, every time the site is built.

Topology lens

Topologically a ball. With n sides to the base: V = 2n, E = 3n, F = n + 2, so V − E + F = 2 for every n. More on the lens.
